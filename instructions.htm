Gameboy Camera WiFi Printer Instructions

Setup

1. Connect the Wi-Fi printer to power via USB C, phone chargers or power banks work fine.

2. Connect your Gameboy via the link cable, making sure the link cable is the correctly orientated and power on the Gameboy.

3. Press the rest button on the printer.

4. Print pictures from the camera as normal.

3. You are able to print pictures singularly or batch print the whole album.

4. Pictures are held indefinitely in the Wi-Fi printers memory, so power can be removed and downloading the photos can be done whenever.

Downloading pictures.

1. Power the Wi-Fi printer via micro USB, for downloading the Gameboy doesn't need to be attached.

2. Search and connect to the internet hotspot created by the Wi-Fi printer via your phone / tablet or PC.

3. When a connection is established, open a new web page and enter the address http://192.168.4.1

4. Select the photos you want to download and hit the download button.

Erasing pictures from the devices memory.

When downloaded pictures can be deleted from the Wi-Fi printers memory through the web browser.

There is no need to delete the pictures straight after download, the device memory should hold more than 200 photos. Keep them on there to share with others or download again at a later date.

Troubleshooting

If your having trouble printing and errors are thrown up by the Gameboy camera, try checking the cables and connections. If the problem persists then the reset button is your best friend, press the reset button on the Wi-Fi printer and wait a few seconds and retry printing.

Wi-Fi problems are generally rectified by tapping the reset button on the device. As you tap the button check for a small blue light near the Wi-Fi antenna, a blink should mean your good to go.
